Testnet practice trove Wave 2

Practice opening a trove on Mezo Testnet using official tools. MUSD Pulse stays read-only — this page never connects a wallet. Follow the links below in your own browser wallet.

  1. Add Mezo Testnet to your wallet: Chain ID 31611, RPC https://rpc.test.mezo.org, explorer explorer.test.mezo.org, native currency BTC. Source: Developer Getting Started.
  2. Request free testnet BTC from the official faucet faucet.test.mezo.org (CAPTCHA protected). FAQ: How do I get testnet BTC?
  3. Optionally add testnet MUSD as a custom token: 0x118917a40FAF1CD7a13dB0Ef56C86De7973Ac503 (18 decimals). Mainnet MUSD stays 0xdD468A1DDc392dcdbEf6db6e34E89AA338F9F186. Source: Contracts reference.
  4. Open the official borrow UI on testnet: mezo.org/feature/borrow. Confirm your wallet is on Mezo Testnet before signing anything.
  5. Borrow against testnet BTC. Protocol floor: at least 1,800 MUSD principal + 200 MUSD gas deposit (≈ 2,000 MUSD total debt on open). Aim for ≥ 150% collateral ratio. Official borrow notes: x402 quickstart Step 2 (testnet borrow) · Borrow against Bitcoin (blog).
  6. Come back here, paste your address under “Check an existing trove”, and compare the live ratio with the planner. Use the Alerts card to draft a reminder to yourself — still no wallet connect from this page.

MUSD across Mezo, Ethereum & Base Wave 2

MUSD is minted on Mezo against BTC collateral. The same ERC-20 address 0xdD468A1DDc392dcdbEf6db6e34E89AA338F9F186 exists on Mezo, Ethereum and Base.

Cross-chain moves use Wormhole Native Token Transfer (NTT) — not a wrapped IOU:

  • Mezo → Ethereum / Base: MUSD is locked on Mezo; an equal amount is minted on the destination.
  • Ethereum / Base → Mezo: MUSD is burned on the source; an equal amount is unlocked on Mezo.

Total supply across the three chains stays consistent under NTT accounting. The “Right now” card shows each chain’s reading; bridged balances can appear on more than one row until you reconcile them.

Official UI options (this page does not bridge for you): Mezo dApp withdraw/deposit, or Portal Bridge. Minimum transfer is typically 0.01 MUSD.

Docs: Wormhole MUSD Bridge · Mainnet bridges overview · Contracts & NTT addresses.
